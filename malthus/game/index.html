What each mechanic really means (say this in your breakout room)
The fieldYour living standard. The 50-yard line is subsistence — the level Malthus says people always get dragged back to.
The blitz 🎮The positive check: when you prosper, population multiplies and food-per-person falls. The better your field position, the harder the hit — that's the whole theory.
Ground & Pound 🏈Agriculture grows food arithmetically — steady but with diminishing returns. Never enough to outrun population alone.
Clock Control ♟️Moral restraint (later marriage) — Malthus's one humane brake. Fewer births, so the blitz is weaker.
Relief ScreenThe Poor Laws: saves people now, but swells the next population wave. Mercy today, deeper trap tomorrow — his most uncomfortable claim.
Hail Mary 🌟The Industrial Revolution — sustained technological growth, the one real escape, which Malthus (writing in 1798) never saw coming. Rare, risky, and only unlocks once you've held prosperity.
